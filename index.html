Wanted: late Toontown assets

I'm rebuilding Toontown Online in the Godot engine. Every asset from my copy of the original game files is converted and running, but those files predate the final game. These 624 pieces were added later, and I haven't found them anywhere yet.

How to help

If you have any of these files, in any format, I'd love a copy. Maya scenes (.mb), Panda3D files (.egg or .bam), textures (.jpg, .rgb, .png) and sounds (.ogg, .mp3, .wav) all help. Names here are the final game's, without the extension. You can reach me on Discord (details at the bottom of the page).

The name's prefix tells you what a file is:

  • tt_m_ model
  • tt_a_ animation
  • tt_r_ rig
  • tt_t_ texture
  • tt_s_ sound
